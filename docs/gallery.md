# Catalogue

Every class in the library, grouped by what it is. **Animations** play when you
click. **Effects** are surface treatments and apply on render, because a glass
panel has nothing to play. **Scroll** is driven by the scrollbar and is
demonstrated properly in the [scroll lab](/scroll-lab).

Nothing autoplays. Use the size control to make the previews bigger; the choice
is remembered.
